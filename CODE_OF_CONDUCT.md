# C38 ImagineWorks — Code of Conduct
*“Born from Making Reality from Your Imagination.”*

This project is a creative and technical space where people build boldly and treat each other with respect. We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and identities. Participation in any C38 ImagineWorks space (GitHub, issues, PRs, discussions, docs, and affiliated chats) means you agree to this Code of Conduct.

---

## Our Standards

### We celebrate:
- **Respectful collaboration** — clear communication, empathy, and patience.
- **Credit and consent** — attribute work, ask before reusing, honor licenses.
- **Constructive feedback** — focus on ideas and code, not people.
- **Accessibility and inclusion** — write docs and UI that are readable and usable.
- **Cultural awareness** — honor names, pronouns, dialects, and lived experience.

### We do not tolerate:
- Harassment, hate speech, or slurs (including racism, colorism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, ageism, xenophobia).
- Doxing, stalking, or threats of violence.
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome advances.
- Deliberate intimidation, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Dismissing or mocking a person’s identity, background, or accent.
- Plagiarism, code theft, or misrepresentation of authorship.

---

## Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces:
- Git repositories, issues, PRs, discussions, and wikis
- Official chats, community calls, social media tied to the project
- In-engine examples, documentation, and demos
- Any public events or showcases run by C38 ImagineWorks

It covers both **behavior** and **content** (text, images, audio, video, and code comments).

---

## Expectations for Maintainers

Maintainers and moderators must:
- Model the standards above.
- Clarify decisions and link to policy when moderating.
- Act on reports promptly, privately, and fairly.
- Recuse themselves when conflicts of interest exist.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:
- **Email:** conduct@c38imagineworks.com (primary)
- **Alternate:** legal@c38imagineworks.com (escalation)

Include:
- What happened (with links/screenshots if possible)
- Where it happened (URL or space)
- When it happened (date/time + timezone)
- Who was involved (GitHub handles or display names)
- Whether you want the report to be confidential to the conduct team

We will acknowledge receipt within **5 business days** and outline next steps. We will keep reporters’ identities confidential to the maximum extent allowed.

---

## Enforcement

Possible actions (progressive, context-based):
1. **Informal reminder** of guidelines with request to adjust behavior
2. **Private warning** from maintainers/moderators
3. **Content moderation** (editing, hiding, locking, or removal)
4. **Temporary participation limits** (issue/PR bans, muted access)
5. **Temporary suspension** from project spaces
6. **Permanent ban** from project spaces and project credit removal
7. **Vendor/platform escalation** if laws or platform policies are violated

Appeals may be sent to **conduct@c38imagineworks.com** within 14 days of notice.

---

## Collaboration Ethics

- **Attribution:** Preserve headers, authorship, and commit history. Do not strip C38 or contributor credits.
- **Authorship honesty:** AI-assisted code or text must be reviewed by a human author and attributed per repository guidelines.
- **Representation:** Do not use project spaces to promote hateful or exploitative content.
- **Licensing:** Follow repository license terms. Ask before using brand marks.

---

## Inclusive Communication

- Use people’s **names and pronouns** correctly.
- Prefer **plain language** in docs and comments.
- Avoid idioms that exclude or demean.
- When corrected, say thanks and adjust — no debate needed.

---

## Anti-Harassment Commitment

C38 ImagineWorks is home to creators from historically marginalized communities. We explicitly protect Black creators and all underrepresented groups from harassment and gatekeeping in our spaces. Violations will be addressed swiftly.
